# Doors — the capacity read

One axis: where the prospect sits relative to their **client-capacity ceiling**.
The door is read from capacity symptoms, and it drives **outreach messaging
only** — it never qualifies a prospect by itself (that's the fundamentals).

| Door | Symptoms that place them here | Screen meaning | Message angle (Layer 3, for later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **overloaded** | "fully booked", waitlist, "not taking clients", turning people away, raising prices, application forms to throttle | Fit door — the **headline** profile | relief, reclaimed time |
| **acquisition-mode** | actively selling, has a program and clients, growing but not visibly maxed | Fit door | scale without hiring; income ceiling without hours ceiling |
| **lead-starved** | lead-gen funnels, "DM me to work together", free-discovery-call CTAs, lead-magnet-heavy, asking about lead tools | **Disqualified for now** — re-engageable | none yet — re-engage when capacity signals appear |

## Rules

- **Door ≠ verdict.** overloaded/acquisition-mode are *fit doors*, but a prospect
  still needs the fit-leaning symptoms (delivery vehicle + documented method) to
  screen `fit`. A door with no fit symptoms is `near-fit`, not `fit`.
- **lead-starved is a sequencing disqualifier** — "not yet," not "never". Tag it
  re-engageable so it re-enters the pipeline when capacity symptoms show up.
- **When capacity is unreadable**, leave the door `—` and let the screen verdict
  stand on the other symptoms. Don't invent a door.

## Why "doors" and not "priority"

Earlier versions of this skill used a High/Low/Nurture *priority* axis keyed to
follower bands. That was a layer-crossing error (Layer-2 heuristic used as a
verdict). Doors replace it: they come straight from capacity **symptoms**, map to
the canonical model, and carry the messaging split for outreach — without ever
pretending follower count is fit.
